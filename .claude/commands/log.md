---
description: Put a conversation on the record - the agreed swap, the lateness chat, the reason behind a decision. In a personal grievance, the record.
---

1. Run `node scripts/roster.mjs note add NAME "what happened"` the moment it is told to you, with the person's own words where they matter.
2. Reading back: `notes NAME`.
3. A note about a decision (a declined request, a removed shift) sits beside the action, not instead of it.
